I'm **Charlie Yang**, a machine-learning engineer at **Amazon Web Services**, where I work on **OpenSearch** — search, retrieval, and neural / vector search at scale. I'm based in Shanghai.

Most of my career has been about retrieval: making search fast and relevant across inverted indexes, vector/k-NN search, neural sparse retrieval, and the systems that serve them. More recently I've been working on **decision-making models and AI agents** — small, *typed* decisions (choose-one, score, yes/no) that need to be quick, well-calibrated, and rigorously evaluated. That work led me to build [**JevBench**](https://github.com/model-collapse/jev-bench), an open, model-agnostic benchmark for typed-decision models.

I write here in two tracks: approachable explainers **[for beginners](/beginners/)**, and technical deep-dives **[for professionals](/professionals/)**.
