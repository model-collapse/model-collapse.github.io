Hi, I’m Charlie Yang 👋

I’m a machine-learning engineer at Amazon Web Services, working on OpenSearch — search, retrieval, and neural / vector search at scale. Based in Shanghai. 🇨🇳

The thread through my work is the same one that runs through good search: given a query and some candidates, make the right call — fast, and for the right reasons. Lately that’s pulled me toward decision-making models and AI agents — small, typed decisions (choose one, score, yes/no) that have to be quick, calibrated, and honestly evaluated. My open-source JevBench benchmark grew out of exactly that itch.

I write about all of it here, on two tracks — pick the one that fits you:

  • 🌱 For AI Beginners — plain-language explainers of ML concepts, intuition and examples first, no jargon left undefined.
  • 🛠️ For AI Professionals — deeper technical write-ups: retrieval, model architecture, calibration, benchmarks, and lessons from shipping real systems.

New here? Start with the beginners track. Building things? Head to the professionals track.
